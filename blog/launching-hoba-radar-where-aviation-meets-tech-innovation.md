Welcome to the official launch of Hoba Radar!

​After weeks of development, we are thrilled to introduce a platform dedicated to the curious minds of the 21st century. Hoba Radar is not just a news site; it is a specialized hub designed to track, analyze, and report on the two pillars of modern engineering: Advanced Technology and Aviation.

​Our Mission

​In an era dominated by Artificial Intelligence and rapid aerospace breakthroughs, Hoba Radar acts as your digital "sensor." We aim to bridge the gap between complex engineering concepts and daily tech updates, providing insights that matter to students, professionals, and enthusiasts alike.

​What to Expect?

​On this platform, we will be covering:

​The Tech Radar: Deep dives into AI, Python automation, and Cloud computing (AWS).

​Aviation Feed: Updates on civil and military aviation, aerodynamics, and the future of flight.

​Project Logs: Exclusive behind-the-scenes looks at our coding projects and mechatronics experiments.

​Why "Radar"?

​Just like a radar scans the horizon for data, we scan the industry for innovation. Whether it’s a new Python library or a breakthrough in jet engine efficiency, you will find it charted here.

​Thank you for joining us on this journey. Buckle up—the flight has just begun!
